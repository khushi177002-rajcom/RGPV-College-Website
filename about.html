About Our University

Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya (RGPV) is a technical university in Madhya Pradesh, India. It focuses on engineering, technology, research and professional education.

Our Mission

To provide quality education and develop knowledgeable, responsible and skilled students.
